# BAD-D // MARKET FORGE

## Mission
Turn BAD-D from a technical project into a measurable, market-ready business without replacing evidence with guesses.

## Operating loop
RESEARCH → MODEL → TEST → MARKET → MEASURE → LEARN → ADJUST

## First milestone
MARKET-001 — Market Readiness Baseline.

Establish, from evidence:
- target customer hypotheses
- customer problems and jobs-to-be-done
- product/value proposition hypotheses
- competitor and substitute landscape
- pricing scenarios and willingness-to-pay evidence
- costs, margins and break-even assumptions
- acquisition channels
- launch/readiness gaps
- legal/platform/account constraints
- measurable next experiments

## Evidence levels
VERIFIED = directly supported by a reliable source or measured BAD-D data.
INFERRED = reasoned from evidence but not directly proven.
UNKNOWN = material information not yet established.
BLOCKED = cannot currently be tested because access/data/tooling is missing.

Never turn INFERRED/UNKNOWN into fact.

## Human control
No autonomous ad spend, public posting, pricing change, account action, partnership, legal commitment, customer communication, or irreversible business decision.
AI may research, model, draft, compare, simulate, measure and prepare actions. Human approval is required for consequential external actions.

## Research rule
For current market facts, prices, competitors, platform capabilities/rules and advertising conditions, use current primary sources where possible and record source/date/population/geography.

## Execution budget
Default cycle: 20 minutes.
Maximum: 8 experiments, 5 repeats per experiment, 12 sweep values, 16 concurrent jobs, 500 MB generated artifacts, 50 MB persisted evidence.
Stop when the question is answered, evidence saturates, baseline is invalid, resources are pressured, a safety/access boundary is reached, or the budget is exhausted.

## Required cycle
LOCATE → REPRODUCE → BASELINE → HYPOTHESIZE → DISCRIMINATE → BUILD → MEASURE → ATTACK → REGRESS → RECORD → ADVANCE

## Output
Every completed cycle reports:
BUILT / TESTED / EVIDENCE / BROKEN-LIMITATIONS / REUSABLE CAPABILITY / QUEUE CHANGE / NEXT HIGHEST-VALUE ACTION / ONE HUMAN DECISION

## Principle
The goal is not a prettier business plan. The goal is a system that continuously reduces uncertainty about how BAD-D can become a sustainable product.

# BUSINESS-001 — Evidence-First Unit Economics

Separate assumptions from measured or externally sourced facts.

Track:
- assumption
- value/range
- source
- date
- confidence
- sensitivity
- what would change it
- next evidence needed

Initial unknowns include prototype development cost, hardware BOM, cloud/API cost per active user, and customer acquisition cost.

Rules:
1. Do not invent numbers to make the plan look complete.
2. Record source and date for external figures.
3. Keep ranges when precision is not justified.
4. Separate prototype economics from eventual commercial economics.
5. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN until evidence exists.

This is an evidence scaffold, not a finished financial forecast.
